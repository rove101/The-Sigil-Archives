# Session 7

Shortly after your success in [[Afral]], a formal, impeccably dressed envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrives. They bear a sealed scroll with the distinctive, ornate seal of High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. The message is flowery, praising your "heroic efforts in stabilizing the town of [[Afral]]" and inviting your group to [[Lyravelle]] to be "personally honored and rewarded" by the Chancellor herself.

On the journey to [[Lyravelle]], a stark contrast emerges to the grim reality of [[Afral]]. As you all venture forth, the landscape becomes more manicured, the roads better maintained. You pass through prosperous villages, their fields tended by Large Rabbits and their skies dotted with well-fed Giant Owls carrying noble passengers.

As the city of [[Lyravelle]] comes into view, you find it indeed lives up to its name. It's a breathtaking metropolis of gleaming white stone, soaring spires, and intricate, almost mesmerizing architecture. Wide, clean boulevards are bustling with elegantly dressed citizens, merchants from distant lands, and the occasional pet cricket are being led through the streets, its chitin a pristine polished blue. The air is filled with the scent of exotic spices, fine perfumes, and the distant, melodic chime of bells. It feels like a beacon of civilization, prosperity, and peace.

# Session 8

The last session marked the final, explosive end of your time in [[Afral]]. You've climbed from the filth of the sewers to the gilded halls of the capital. Your Wizard friend, [[Ignis Vayne|Master Ignis]], revealed the ledger you stole was a documented network of treason pointing to a more complex web of near-do-wells. It confirmed that the merchant [[Nadar]] was merely something called a "Weaver", likely someone low ranked in this mysterious cult, taking orders from a higher power known as 'The Spinner.' Before you could act on this, an envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrived, bringing a summons from High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. You accepted the invitation, leaving the grim yet hopeful people of [[Afral]] behind for a journey through the prosperous, manicured heart of the Republic.

Arriving in [[Lyravelle]], you were thrust immediately into the center of attention. After a brief rest, you attended a grand feast held in your honor, where the Chancellor, Lady Valerius, delivered a public speech. She reframed your chaotic actions—the explosions, the sewage, the fights—as "vigilance and civic courage," solidifying your heroic status while carefully sanitizing the official record. You met the most powerful figures in the Republic, including the imposing Lord General [[Kaelen Volkov]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]]’s own estranged family, House Calvain. The evening ended with the Chancellor awarding you a sack of gold and the deed to a townhouse, a magnificent reward that serves as a beautiful, very public anchor in the very city you find yourselves in.

# Session 9

After a harrowing battle with some cultists, a demonic presence was revealed to you calling himself “the bringer of chaos”. After taunting the party, the demon unleashed his shadow to decimate the material plane. Following it to the streets, you all rushed inside the Manor house of the Kestrels. Inside you find several eerie hauntings happening. Reality warping on a whim, light playing tricks on you, cutlery coming to life and attacking. Fending off the strange happenings of the haunted house leads you all to the attic and face to face with the shadow draped in red. After dealing devastating blows to the creature, it split into two deadly forces. A battle hardened skeleton wielding a black metal sword clad in armor and a red vapor who drains the very essence of life from its foes. The skeleton was no match for the blade of [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] and the divine judgment of Brother E’tellor, falling to their strikes. It was too late however, [[Noxervus]] having been sapped of all her life force was on borrowed time, and that clock was ticking.

# Session 10

A gentle fragrance of sweet citrusy with hints of vanilla and spice wafts through the air. The green walls have a soft organic look to them, like the stem of a flower. Light filters through windows made of petals casting hues of pink and orange along the floor. Small tree-like creatures wander the room, bringing nectar that tastes like sunshine and tea with the aroma of mint and rain. You begin to remember why you are here.

A tall slender Owlin stands over the three of you, his feathers leaking wisps of smoke in the sunlight. He has changed from the rather simple grey travelers cloak you first met him in into a much more extravagant outfit. His legs clad in long slate pinstriped pants and a plain off white shirt across his chest. Draped over his shoulders is a cape woven of roses and Midnight Petunias. The deep red and even darker purples draw the attention to his face. His beak hooks into a wicked point and as black as the night itself. Feathers around his face hold that same off white color creating an almost mask-like appearance. Decades of wisdom swirl like mist in his eyes as they remain trained on [[Noxervus]]. His gaze is soft.

# Session 11

Having beaten the corrupt shadows infesting [[Noxervus]]' mind you all took a well deserved rest in [[EverBloom]], a domain ruled by a powerful Archfey called Aetherguard. During your week of rest and recuperation, you all decided to split up. [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]], having been given a tantalizing hint at his forgotten past, decided to use his favor and ask [[Umbrax]], the Veiled Prince, what he knows. [[Umbrax]] promised him three answers of which [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] asked two questions, gaining much information about the name Faelar and discovering his ancient connections to the land itself. [[Verso Calvain|Verso]], intrigued by the Veil’s misty substance, wandered into it without a guide. As he walked deeper, a pair of glowing eyes and a lantern with a steady flame appeared in the fog. Reaching out, he was grabbed by this creature but a sense of familiarity washed over him. [[Umbrax]], sensing this malign threat, pushed [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] and [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] back to [[EverBloom]] leaving [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] with the sense of a warm hug that pushed him to tears.

[[Noxervus]], having gotten used to her new muscular frame, began to wander [[EverBloom]] and reconnect with old friends, opting to stop by and have a small chat to show she was in town before meeting with the Archfey herself. Nearing the end of their week, she decided to find a pair of mischievous goblin-like creatures calling themselves Grinny and Glim. After their usual tricks and several minutes wasted, they gave [[Noxervus]] a “magical” compass that points to a Giant, who may or may not exist, capable of forging powerful runic weapons.\
After many goodbyes, [[Umbrax]] parted the Veil back to The Basin, your home plane of existence. Winding up on the side of Lodestone mountain, a portal opened revealing Taniya Eldriada, the Right Hand of the republic, and a powerful arcane caster called [[Kaelus Vayne|Master Vayne]]. Ms. Eldriada explained your new assignment, to capture or kill Lord Commander Daleram Berdanak for high treason. Fearing something was not right, she implored that he be brought in alive. With a new set of winter clothes to handle most of the mountain's elements, the party made their way into [[Ironpeak]] and rented a room for the night at The Red Herring Inn.

# Session 12

You wake up to the glint of morning sun filtering through frost-tinted windows. The howling wind outside, softened to a low whistle by the magically maintained calm within [[Ironpeak]]'s walls.

You find yourselves congregating in the double room where [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] slept. The air here is thick with the smell of old pine and the lingering warmth of the fire flowing through the halls of the Red Herring Inn. A sharp contrast to the biting cold you trekked through yesterday to get here.

The Commander is holed up in Marheim-Clad keep, surrounded by an army of magically enhanced and resistant Wardens. You are three strangers in a city of stone and steel.

So, as you check your gear—[[Verso Calvain|Verso]], perhaps feeling the heavy pull of the new blade at your hip; [[Noxervus]], stretching muscles that feel stronger than they did a week ago; and [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] your mind reeling from things that were and still could be—what is the plan? How do you intend to get close to a careful dwarf who you intend on capturing or killing?

# Session 13

You wake to cold cramped conditions. The only light is a small flickering candle at your bedside. Leaving your confined corners, the three of you meet just outside the mundane flaps of the sleeping quarters of Mining camp Wacot. The Stalagmites glow faintly with luminous moss casting long black shadows. The sound of Sun-Heart Plums being freshly squeezed pulls your attention to the group of miners readying for a trip back to the surface.
