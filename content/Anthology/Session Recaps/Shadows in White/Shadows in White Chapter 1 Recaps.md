# Session 1

The party successfully, albeit messily, infiltrated the Merchant’s Guild warehouse in Afral. Verso, having been discovered, excused himself to go meet with the local merchant leader leading to a more direct approach. The party soon found themselves outmatched and trapped between two towering thugs. Fighting your way out of the leader’s office and making your way to the exit door, you notice Wing barely holding onto life. Cutting his bindings the four of you make your escape into the night. Regrouping at the Red Tigress Inn, a new plan will need to be made if the Lunar Dust plaguing this town is to be stopped.

# Session 2

You had revealed yourselves to the local corrupt merchants selling Lunar Dust. After finding out the ring leader, a man named Narder, placed an order for armor and weapons using his contacts, a new plan was concocted. Avoiding the main streets, fearing patrols of mercenaries, you made your way to a local side street. There you met with a handful of older merchants who are bothered by the Merchant Guild's presents. Verso managed to rile up a seamstress into believing they were against the Guild. After taking some measurements, he invited you to a little get together where you later learned that Narder uses rough cloth bags to transport and spread information to his subordinates. After a night of drinking, cards, and idle chit chat, the three of you returned to the Red Tigris Inn. After coming up with a plan to have Wing convince the guards to patrol a few of these locations, the three of you retired to your rooms in preparation for tomorrow's action.

# Session 3

After successfully making your way inside the shop, Verso began to distract the store owner Ellen. Elred and Noxervus, in the meantime, began fiddling with strange statues. Examining the plaques they realized they were all jumbled up. Suddenly a frantic halfling burst upstairs, stumbling over her words about a magic gnome. Ellen excused himself leaving the party to study the statues even more. After several minutes of twisting and turning, pulling and pushing. The statues spoke and an audible click was heard across the room revealing a secret door. There you found several valuable items as well as maps detailing the routes not only in the sewers and buildings in town but also the ancient tunnels deep beneath Afral. Paying for several magical items and quickly making your way outside, you find a sewer entrance and head underground to blow up the store’s access to the sewers. Elred activated the bomb with enough time for everyone to make it to the Red Tigress Inn through the sewers.

# Session 4

The air is thick with the sweet smell of success. Escaping from the ancient mining tunnels that lie below to a different kind of darkness—the grimy sewers of Afral. You have also defeated the psychic ooze and in the process, found a strange crystal at its core. You have an inkling of what the crystal does but intend to deliver it to your Wizard friend, Master Ignis, who will decipher its mysteries. The city above you is in a state of unease. The Merchants' Guild's power is crumbling. The game has changed, and you are at the center of it.

# Session 5

Over the course of the week following the well cleansing and the tunnels being sealed, Afral undergoes a noticeable and profound transformation. The change isn't instant—there are still rubble and repair crews—but the spirit of the town is lifted.

You notice children running freely in the streets, their mothers no longer clutching them with fevered anxiety. The beer at the Red Tigress Inn tastes crisper, and the bread baked by the gnome, Kaelia, seems to have a new, robust quality. The ever-present, low-grade malaise that once seemed to hang over the common folk has lifted.

Durgan Stonehand the blacksmith is working double shifts, his forge roaring. When you pass his shop, he gives you a curt, genuine nod of respect. You overhear Harlen proudly refusing an unreasonable delivery price, smiling as he knows the Guild can't retaliate. Prices for basic goods have fallen, reflecting the return to honest trade.

The remaining City Guards are visibly patrolling, but their focus is on rebuilding trust, not the corruption. They offer polite greetings and occasionally ask the party for input on damaged areas. The anxiety in the air is replaced by cautious optimism—the people know you fought for this peace.

Thanks to Ak’arr and Master Ignis spreading your doings, you are no longer seen as "barbaric thugs" or “Mercanaries.” The common folk whisper your names with admiration, calling you the "The Town-Setters" or "The Well-Saviors." They speak openly about the Merchant's Guild leader and his crew being arrested, a freedom of speech that was impossible just a week prior.

# Session 6?

Having settled into your new house, you all decided to go your separate ways and explore the city. Elred ventured to the Whispering Waters Fountain. There he began to hear a faint voice in the distance but couldn’t make out what was being said. After walking around the circumference he settled down on a nearby bench and spent the day relaxing to the sound of the fountain. Verso, on the other hand, decided to be more proactive and spend some of his reward money. He found himself in front of a shop called Grand Emporium of Antiquities. Inside he procured several mysterious orbs said to protect those from the elements as well as various bits and bobs. Noxervus, seeking a more thorough way to unwind, found a small shop called The Steamy Smithy Blacksmith and Day Spa, run by two Dwarves, Eager and Esme Everlong. Inside she bathed with some soaps smelling of her favorite scent, fresh cut grass.

Returning home having idle chit-chat, a rapid knock was heard at the door. Verso opened the door to find a Priest of the Doctrine of the Ascendant soul. He began speaking quickly begging the heroes to come with him. After easing his pace, he began to tell of a murder. The party rushed over to a small cottage and found an older woman posed on her knees, her house a mess. After investigating, they find a small shard of obsidian embedded within the doorframe. Brother E’tellor, tells the party of a contact inside the Messengers Guild who Ms. Cyrille worked with.

Arriving at the Messengers Guild, Mr. Lloyal Carter greets you all and takes you back to a private room. Noxervus takes the lead asking questions and finding many useful bits of information, including a Cartographer friend who just so happens to be a hobby Star-Charter. Turning to leave Mr. Carter imparts one final task. "Find that box. It should still be in her cottage. It won't be easy to find—Cyrille was always a champ at hide and seek."
