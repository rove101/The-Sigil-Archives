# Session 7

Shortly after your success in [[Afral]], a formal, impeccably dressed envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrives. They bear a sealed scroll with the distinctive, ornate seal of High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. The message is flowery, praising your "heroic efforts in stabilizing the town of [[Afral]]" and inviting your group to [[Lyravelle]] to be "personally honored and rewarded" by the Chancellor herself.

On the journey to [[Lyravelle]], a stark contrast emerges to the grim reality of [[Afral]]. As you all venture forth, the landscape becomes more manicured, the roads better maintained. You pass through prosperous villages, their fields tended by Large Rabbits and their skies dotted with well-fed Giant Owls carrying noble passengers.

As the city of [[Lyravelle]] comes into view, you find it indeed lives up to its name. It's a breathtaking metropolis of gleaming white stone, soaring spires, and intricate, almost mesmerizing architecture. Wide, clean boulevards are bustling with elegantly dressed citizens, merchants from distant lands, and the occasional pet cricket are being led through the streets, its chitin a pristine polished blue. The air is filled with the scent of exotic spices, fine perfumes, and the distant, melodic chime of bells. It feels like a beacon of civilization, prosperity, and peace.

# Session 8

The last session marked the final, explosive end of your time in [[Afral]]. You've climbed from the filth of the sewers to the gilded halls of the capital. Your Wizard friend, [[Ignis Vayne|Master Ignis]], revealed the ledger you stole was a documented network of treason pointing to a more complex web of near-do-wells. It confirmed that the merchant [[Nadar]] was merely something called a "Weaver", likely someone low ranked in this mysterious cult, taking orders from a higher power known as 'The Spinner.' Before you could act on this, an envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrived, bringing a summons from High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. You accepted the invitation, leaving the grim yet hopeful people of [[Afral]] behind for a journey through the prosperous, manicured heart of the Republic.

Arriving in [[Lyravelle]], you were thrust immediately into the center of attention. After a brief rest, you attended a grand feast held in your honor, where the Chancellor, Lady Valerius, delivered a public speech. She reframed your chaotic actions—the explosions, the sewage, the fights—as "vigilance and civic courage," solidifying your heroic status while carefully sanitizing the official record. You met the most powerful figures in the Republic, including the imposing Lord General [[Kaelen Volkov]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]]’s own estranged family, House Calvain. The evening ended with the Chancellor awarding you a sack of gold and the deed to a townhouse, a magnificent reward that serves as a beautiful, very public anchor in the very city you find yourselves in.

# Session 9

After a harrowing battle with some cultists, a demonic presence was revealed to you calling himself “the bringer of chaos”. After taunting the party, the demon unleashed his shadow to decimate the material plane. Following it to the streets, you all rushed inside the Manor house of the Kestrels. Inside you find several eerie hauntings happening. Reality warping on a whim, light playing tricks on you, cutlery coming to life and attacking. Fending off the strange happenings of the haunted house leads you all to the attic and face to face with the shadow draped in red. After dealing devastating blows to the creature, it split into two deadly forces. A battle hardened skeleton wielding a black metal sword clad in armor and a red vapor who drains the very essence of life from its foes. The skeleton was no match for the blade of [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] and the divine judgment of Brother E’tellor, falling to their strikes. It was too late however, [[Noxervus]] having been sapped of all her life force was on borrowed time, and that clock was ticking.

# Session 10

A gentle fragrance of sweet citrusy with hints of vanilla and spice wafts through the air. The green walls have a soft organic look to them, like the stem of a flower. Light filters through windows made of petals casting hues of pink and orange along the floor. Small tree-like creatures wander the room, bringing nectar that tastes like sunshine and tea with the aroma of mint and rain. You begin to remember why you are here.

A tall slender Owlin stands over the three of you, his feathers leaking wisps of smoke in the sunlight. He has changed from the rather simple grey travelers cloak you first met him in into a much more extravagant outfit. His legs clad in long slate pinstriped pants and a plain off white shirt across his chest. Draped over his shoulders is a cape woven of roses and Midnight Petunias. The deep red and even darker purples draw the attention to his face. His beak hooks into a wicked point and as black as the night itself. Feathers around his face hold that same off white color creating an almost mask-like appearance. Decades of wisdom swirl like mist in his eyes as they remain trained on [[Noxervus]]. His gaze is soft.

# Session 11

Having beaten the corrupt shadows infesting [[Noxervus]]' mind you all took a well deserved rest in [[EverBloom]], a domain ruled by a powerful Archfey called Aetherguard. During your week of rest and recuperation, you all decided to split up. [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]], having been given a tantalizing hint at his forgotten past, decided to use his favor and ask [[Umbrax]], the Veiled Prince, what he knows. [[Umbrax]] promised him three answers of which [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] asked two questions, gaining much information about the name Faelar and discovering his ancient connections to the land itself. [[Verso Calvain|Verso]], intrigued by the Veil’s misty substance, wandered into it without a guide. As he walked deeper, a pair of glowing eyes and a lantern with a steady flame appeared in the fog. Reaching out, he was grabbed by this creature but a sense of familiarity washed over him. [[Umbrax]], sensing this malign threat, pushed [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] and [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] back to [[EverBloom]] leaving [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] with the sense of a warm hug that pushed him to tears.

[[Noxervus]], having gotten used to her new muscular frame, began to wander [[EverBloom]] and reconnect with old friends, opting to stop by and have a small chat to show she was in town before meeting with the Archfey herself. Nearing the end of their week, she decided to find a pair of mischievous goblin-like creatures calling themselves Grinny and Glim. After their usual tricks and several minutes wasted, they gave [[Noxervus]] a “magical” compass that points to a Giant, who may or may not exist, capable of forging powerful runic weapons.\
After many goodbyes, [[Umbrax]] parted the Veil back to The Basin, your home plane of existence. Winding up on the side of Lodestone mountain, a portal opened revealing Taniya Eldriada, the Right Hand of the republic, and a powerful arcane caster called [[Kaelus Vayne|Master Vayne]]. Ms. Eldriada explained your new assignment, to capture or kill Lord Commander Daleram Berdanak for high treason. Fearing something was not right, she implored that he be brought in alive. With a new set of winter clothes to handle most of the mountain's elements, the party made their way into [[Ironpeak]] and rented a room for the night at The Red Herring Inn.

# Session 12

You wake up to the glint of morning sun filtering through frost-tinted windows. The howling wind outside, softened to a low whistle by the magically maintained calm within [[Ironpeak]]'s walls.

You find yourselves congregating in the double room where [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] slept. The air here is thick with the smell of old pine and the lingering warmth of the fire flowing through the halls of the Red Herring Inn. A sharp contrast to the biting cold you trekked through yesterday to get here.

The Commander is holed up in Marheim-Clad keep, surrounded by an army of magically enhanced and resistant Wardens. You are three strangers in a city of stone and steel.

So, as you check your gear—[[Verso Calvain|Verso]], perhaps feeling the heavy pull of the new blade at your hip; [[Noxervus]], stretching muscles that feel stronger than they did a week ago; and [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] your mind reeling from things that were and still could be—what is the plan? How do you intend to get close to a careful dwarf who you intend on capturing or killing?

# Session 18

The bright glow of the morning sun having shown through thin windows, our brave adventures began to rouse from their sleep. Their long trek through the mines and the discovery of a tunnel connecting to [[Ironpeak#Ashen Quarter|Ashen Quarter]] leading to more questions than answers. Having been originally tasked with apprehending [[Dalram Berdanak|Commander Dalram Berdanak]], this straightforward mission soon became clouded by several hurdles blocking the way. After readying themselves, Verso made his way to the bar. It was as lively as always, with the smell of seared meats and lavender, the warmth of a crackling fire lighting up the simple wooden tables filled with people from all walks of life. Speaking with [[Calley O'Toole]], a fresh batch of bread would be brought to their room. Unbeknownst to him, four sets of eyes were watched him return to his room.

The topic quickly turned to what they have learned from a semi friendly ghost and several papers preserved by the cold stale cavern air. [[Noxervus]] set the papers down so all could observe, and they set about fitting the puzzle pieces together. A loud crash was heard outside ending their conversation. A towering Ork man lay just outside the door. Brushing himself off, he took his mysterious crystal from [[Verso Calvain|Verso]], muttered a thanks and turned the corner.

Shrugging off the strange interaction, [[Noxervus]] retrieved the papers and began to leave the [[Ironpeak.md#Red Herring Inn|Red Herring Inn]]. Instead of a simple passing encounter, a man quickly plunged a poisoned dagger deep into her side, severely wounding her. As the ambush unfolded, the Ork from earlier began slinging spells at Verso. [Elred](Elred%20Pergwyn.md), stuck in between his allies began to provide support. It was during this divided chaos that an avian figure suddenly rushed up next to the man attacking [[Noxervus]]. Striking with a hooked blade, the avian landed a painful blow, successfully catching the documents and ripping them from her pocket.

The assailants long gone, we rejoin our heroes as they lick their wounds. The knowledge that they lost giving them realization that their next steps should be spent in resting, research and remarks with the locals. And who better than with a collector of knowledge and new friend, [[Liroliel Vitarium]] from the library.

As you sit there reading some notes related to planar travel, you hear the door swing open and the jingle of a small bell. Your colleague, [[Liroliel Vitarium|Mrs. Vitarium]] excuses herself to tend to the library. You'd rather keep to your self in this already cramped and unorganized space, but you have a duty to the [Order of Silent Scholars](Obsidians%20Gate.md#Order%20of%20Silent%20Scholars). You groan, your wings stiff from being hunched over your research for who knows how long. You stand to make your way past the curtain and are greeted by three adventurous looking people. A tall human with hair in a tight bun and pale skin, a muscular Harengon with sharp amber eyes and large antlers adorning her head, and a wood elf draped in a thick green cloak with a fancy hand bag containing an ant at their side.

Having spent their time at the library researching their attackers, our heroes uncovered the Spiders' past exploits, such as how they once broke a blockade on the town of [[Ember Meadows]]. Amidst this research, the original party members made a conscious choice to keep their objective a secret from their newest ally, a purple winged tiefling calling themselves Sam.

During this downtime, Sam returned deeper into the library to retrieve some personal belongings. Noxervus, having requested a book on the insectoid Thri-Kreen to distract the timid librarian, learned about their culture and if there is any way they can be delt with. Eventually, the group split up to cover more ground: Verso chose to stay behind at the library, while Sam, Elred, and Noxervus headed to the Null-Forges.

At the forges, the trio reconnected with the forge master and had several pertinent questions answered; though they are still sifting through what was actually helpful. They promise to help hunt down a Frost Troll and retrieve its Ice Heart in the coming days, but a lingering concern hangs over the group regarding the current whereabouts of the three assassins who ambushed them.

Now entering the second day of their long rest, the party continues to collect information and pursue personal goals. Our harmonious harengon leaves the group looking to find work for extra money and plans to speak with Verso privately. The twiggy Teifling sets off to investigate the Red Herring Inn while our headstrong human does his own investigating. Leaving an earnest elf to wander the town by himself.

# Session 20

Before leaving the safety of Ironpeak, the party spent time wrapping up a few personal investigations. Sam spoke with Calley O'Toole at the Red Herring Inn, discovering that a mysterious woman in the left balcony room holds more information. Meanwhile, Elred established a new contact with a young street-smart Tiefling named Miska. Verso also spent a hefty 75 gold on a book to learn Sylvan, while coming to a stark realization about the contract bound to his arm.

Setting out into the freezing wilderness to hunt the Frost Troll, the party struggled against the harsh elements. Despite their best efforts—Elred's Troll knowledge proving less than helpful—the group lost their way in the snow for seven grueling hours. Their wandering eventually pulled them into a brutal ambush by a pack of Ice Kobolds. The skirmish was chaotic, pitting the party against a coordinated strike team of Kobold Hunters, Shamans, and a particularly evasive Trapper.

With [[Brunhilda Gnarlzahk|Brunhilda]] holding the line and the Vitarium twins expending several of their spell slots to provide magical support, the entourage survived the icy assault. Searching the fallen kobolds, the group secured a highly valuable and bizarre haul of loot: A pair of spell scrolls, an incredibly durable Spellbook, a Crossbow with a cangle attached to the front, a Potion of Climbing, a brass timepiece, and some trinkets including toes and a necklace of bones.

After the battle, the exhausted group set up camp. During this lull in action, [[Sammael|Sam]] questioned [[Liroliel Vitarium|Liroliel]] about a man named [[Marek Ranemdel Clyde]], learning she had seen someone matching his description three months prior. The cold night also brought a moment of quiet vulnerability. On the edge of camp, Noxervus and Verso spoke privately about the dark pact. In a surprising confession, Verso revealed that despite the ominous nature of his patron, he actually revels in the power granted to him and deeply enjoys finally having a place where he feels like he belongs.

# Session 21

Having woken to the freezing air of Loadstone Mountain, our intrepid heroes are reminded of the unforgiving environment they find themselves in.

[[Brunhilda Gnarlzahk|Brunhilda]], concerned they don't fully grasp the threat they are to find, offered the party some substantial aid, distributing three Potions of Fortitude to [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]], [[Verso Calvain|Verso]], and [[Sammael|Sam]]. Meanwhile, [[Liroliel Vitarium|Liroliel]] was understandably hesitant about facing the dangerous cavern. However, the party managed to convince the quiet librarian to brave the cave alongside her brother to help them on their mission.

With their entourage ready, they finally ventured into the icy depths. Utilizing his wings, [[Sammael]] took point and scouted ahead of the main group. During his reconnaissance, he discovered an area featuring a table scattered with various bottles and strewn plates. The air remained still as he looked around before returning, finding no sign of monsters.

The exploration wasn't without its hazards, however. While investigating the cave, [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] attempted to open a chest that turned out to be trapped, triggering a mechanism that sent rocks falling down on him. Despite the trap, the party managed to secure and share the loot among themselves before pressing deeper into the Frost Troll's den.

Upon entering the den, the group was met with absolute chaos. The massive Frost Troll immediately began its attack, while at the same time, relentless waves of Ice Wraiths flung themselves from the mysterious portal.

Having proven victorious, the party scouted the large open room finding a soon to be unlocked chest filled with loot before turning to leave.

# Session 23

After finalizing their infiltration plan with [[Brunhilda Gnarlzahk]] and securing the blueprints to Marheim-Clad Keep, the party inexplicably lost consciousness while sitting around the table inside the [[Ironpeak#Null Forges|Null Forges]]. The blistering heat of the forge faded away, replaced instantly by the suffocating scent of burning ash, the rushing of sand on desert air currents, and the deafening roar of a brutal clash.

They awoke to find themselves thrust into a vision of the past: the Battle of Obsidian Pass. Integrated into the ranks of an old military platoon, the party was shocked to discover that their commanding officer was none other than a younger, more sure [[Dalram Berdanak]]. As they fought off wave after wave of enemy forces, the party witnessed a vastly different side of their current target—a fiercely loyal and honorable leader who valued bravery and refused to treat his soldiers as disposable.

Amidst the grueling skirmishes, [[Dalram Berdanak|Commander Dalram]] delivered a rousing speech, rallying the party to hold the line. However, the tide of the battle dramatically shifted when [[Kaelen Volkov|General Volkov]] unexpectedly emerged from a nearby cavern. Just as he appeared, the enemy launched a devastating battery of magical attacks against the ravine, causing massive boulders to violently collapse around the Vanguard's forces.

Cutting through the destruction, [[Kaelen Volkov]] delivered a desperate, urgent order to [[Dalram Berdanak|Dalram]] and the party: the enemy forces were attempting to breach The Substratum—the deeply hidden sepulcher within [[Obsidians Gate]]. They were commanded to march on the library and protect it at all costs before its esoteric secrets could fall into the wrong hands.

# Session 25

The daring escape from [[Ironpeak]] began precariously as the party slipped out the window of [[Dalram Berdanak|Commander Dalram's]] office, with the exhausted dwarf nearly plummeting during the tense crossing to the Keep's outer wall. Once down in the shadowed streets, the group needed to bypass the heavy guard patrols. [[Noxervus]] quickly improvised, tossing four silver pieces to a local street kid to cause a scene. The child delivered masterfully, tossing a bottle down a distant alleyway and knocking over a cart full of stale bread and musty ale to draw the remaining guards away, clearing the path to the sewers.

Navigating the damp, echoing tunnels of the under-city, the party approached the resistance's trials. At the first arcane door, they successfully deduced that the answer was literal silence, though in their focus, they entirely missed the ominous dripping of water from a large crack in the ceiling above. Pushing forward, they encountered a puzzle room featuring a Cleric, Knight, and Hag puppet. Through trial and error, they managed to match the puppets to their appropriate costumes. However, when [[Sammael|Sam]] pushed the Hag, the puppet violently animated and attacked. The group quickly dismantled the construct, retrieving a key that popped out of its chest to unlock the path forward.

The initiation culminated in an old overflow aqueduct. Immersed in the sound of rushing water, the party grew highly suspicious of the damp room and its aquatic-themed loot. Their caution paid off when they located a single, perfectly dry, sealed envelope. By speaking the answer to the riddle aloud, the final door popped open. Believing the room to be solved and safe, [[Sammael|Sam]] eagerly scooped up as much of the loot as he could carry, leaving behind only a simple coin pouch bearing a griffon motif, which [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] quietly claimed.

Safely sealed within the hidden sanctuary, the decades of tension finally fell from [[Dalram Berdanak|Dalram's]] shoulders. Speaking freely at last, he admitted that while he desperately wished to tell his estranged daughter about their familial bond, he needed to remain a "ghost" to keep her safe. When asked who could have orchestrated their painful separation, [[Dalram Berdanak|Dalram]] pointed the finger at a single, powerful figure: the Republic's wizard, [[Kaelus Vayne]].

Before the party could fully process this revelation, [[Sammael|Sam]] spotted a small, marble-like object resting on a recessed pedestal. His blood boiled and his runic scars flared with sudden understanding—it was a Way-finder's Key, a direct connection to [[Marek Ranemdel Clyde|Marek]] and a way to track him down. As his mind drifted to the many planes he had visited, Sam reached out and grabbed the object. Instantly, the group was violently sucked into a disorienting vortex of spiraling colors and sensations. Feeling the very order of nature tearing away around them, [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] tapped into a massive surge of primal power, reaching out to anchor their wild planar travel. Thanks to [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]]'s intervention, the chaotic portal spat the party out, crashing them down into an unknown, grassy meadow.

# Session 27

Arriving in the bustling town of [[TradeCross]], the party was immediately thrust into chaos as panicked refugees flooded the streets, fleeing from a mysterious mist that animated everyday objects. Over thirty desperate civilians were frantically trying to seek shelter inside the [[TradeCross#Mill Hill Tavern|Mill Hill Tavern]] while the town's bailiff, [[Axel Cobblepot]], struggled to maintain order. Springing into action, the party successfully protected the civilians and helped secure the square. Grateful for their intervention, Bailiff Cobblepot approached the heroes, promising them a handsome reward of 100 gold pieces each if they could find the source of the animating mists and put an end to the threat.

Seeking more information, the group entered the [[TradeCross#Mill Hill Tavern|Mill Hill Tavern]] and sought out a young tiefling named [[Elspeth Arcade]], who was suspected to know something about the strange occurrences. When [[Sammael|Sam]] aggressively confronted [[Elspeth Arcade|Elspeth]], the protective tavern keeper, [[Hilde Mundle 1]], stepped in to de-escalate the situation. Once tempers cooled, [[Hilde Mundle 1|Ms. Mundle]] revealed she was hiding a loud, magical sack that belonged to the eccentric wizard [[Nimbus Goldenhand]], whose tower lay to the north. Eager to leave her mistakes behind, [[Elspeth Arcade|Elspeth]] agreed to reveal what she knew and help the party locate their missing friend, [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]], in exchange for safe passage to the prestigious [[Silverwood#Silverleaf Athenaeum|Silverleaf Athenaeum]] of [[Silverwood]].

Following the leads pointing toward the wizard's tower, the party traveled north into the thickening fog, walking past families rushing to safety in [[TradeCross]]. Along the way, they stumbled upon a lone cottage with a broken window and scattered feathers around the back. Prying up the floorboards, [[Noxervus]] discovered a pair of dwarven siblings fending off a vicious swarm of animated silver cutlery. The dwarves admitted to helping the wizard's apprentice plunder the supposedly defenseless tower. Inside the cottage, [[Sammael|Sam]] investigated a strange statue of a Half-Orc clutching a heavy tome, quickly realizing it was the petrified apprentice, [[Gentry Thorne]], holding [[Nimbus Goldenhand]]'s stolen spellbook. Using a scroll of greater restoration from the magic rucksack, [[Sammael|Sam]] cured the petrified apprentice, freeing him from his stony prison.

Having retrieved the stolen spellbook, the party finally departed for [[Nimbus Goldenhand]]'s tower, only to find that the animated objects had formed a formidable barricade around the structure. After deliberating their plan of attack, the party decided to simply return the stolen spellbook to the objects, a gesture that pleased the defenders and earned them safe passage inside. Navigating the lavish velvet interior of the tower, they ignored the bizarre sight of a house cat being put on trial and descended the meek spiral stairs into the basement. In the center of the underground chamber, they found a large alchemical device pumping gallons of thick mist into glass pipes, spreading the animating magic across the surrounding lands.

Determined to stop the chaos, [[Noxervus]] attempted to smash the mist-producing structure while [[Sammael|Sam]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] furiously fended off scuttling swarms of sharp glass shards, one of which even enveloped [[Sammael|Sam]]. Having sensed the commotion inside his sanctum, [[Nimbus Goldenhand]] stepped through a portal and dispelled the chaos around them. After a brief chat, the wizard rewarded [[Sammael|Sam]] with his own magical sack and tasked the party with returning his unruly apprentice, [[Gentry Thorne|Gentry]], to his older brother [[Corvin Thorne]] in [[Silverwood]]. Returning to [[TradeCross]] victorious with [[Gentry Thorne|Gentry]] in tow, the trio was quickly found by [[Elspeth Arcade|Elspeth]], who had [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] following close behind. Finally reunited, the entire party gathered back at the Mill Hill Tavern, regaling the grateful townsfolk with the tale of their courageous story.

# Session 29

The crisp morning light filters through the windows of the "Two Doors Tavern," finally chasing away the unnatural chill of last night and allowing you to reflect on the whirlwind of your arrival in [[Silverwood]]. Yesterday, after navigating the glowing stone walkways of the magic capital, you brought [[Gentry Thorne|Gentry]] and [[Elspeth Arcade|Elspeth]] to the [[Silverwood#Silverleaf Athenaeum|Silverleaf Athenaeum]]. You handed your prisoner over to [[Corvin Thorne]]—who casually cast a silencing illusion over [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] for trying to speak in [[Gentry Thorne|Gentry]]'s defense—and [[Sammael|Sam]] consulted the meticulous administrator about his volatile Waystone Key.

With your bounties delivered, the party split. [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] and [[Sammael|Sam]] descended into the humming laboratories to reunite with the eccentric artificer [[Tomel Wren]], who fixed [[Verso Calvain|Verso]]'s arm and marveled at the raw power of your strange magic batteries. He advised keeping them close and whispered a rumor of a crashed "metal flying creature" in the wilds that might hold more. Meanwhile, amidst the glowing cityscape, [[Noxervus]] and [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] took a quiet moment to help [[Elspeth Arcade|Elspeth]] draft a letter home to [[Hilde Mundle|Hilde]], while [[Noxervus]] penned her own heartfelt message offering kinship to [[Chardane Starview]].

As the mist swallowed the lantern light, reality itself seemed to stutter—as Noxervus suddenly found herself walking down the path, before desperately running back inside the "correct" tavern door. Now, with the sun up and the only fog remaining that of dew drops, the streets of [[Silverwood]] await your next move.

# Session 30

The crisp morning air of [[Silverwood]] proved to be full of surprises! Waking to a warm meal, our brave heroes decided on a plan for the day, starting with visiting [[Corvin Thorne|Corvin]] to finally uncover the truth behind the [[Afral]] purification crystal. [[Corvin Thorne|Corvin]] proudly explained its noble purpose—providing drinkable water for seafaring vessels—and eagerly escorted you into a secret archive housing projects personally overseen by the Arch-Mage. However, upon discovering the blueprints for the device were missing, [[Corvin Thorne|Corvin]] was more than willing to arranged a direct meeting with [[Herric Wranfaren Vistees|Arch-Mage Herric]] himself!

To kill some time before the big meeting, the party hit the shops. [[Elred Pergwyn|Elred]] used his mercantile charm to score a fantastic deal selling off some spare potions, while [[Sammael|Sam]] successfully fenced some magical aquatic armor to a local elf blacksmith.
