# The Spider's Invitation

### The Summons to [[Lyravelle]]

Shortly after your success in [[Afral]], a formal, impeccably dressed envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrives. They bear a sealed scroll with the distinctive, ornate seal of High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. The message is flowery, praising your "heroic efforts in stabilizing the town of [[Afral]]" and inviting your group to [[Lyravelle]] to be "personally honored and rewarded" by the Chancellor herself.

On the journey to [[Lyravelle]], a stark contrast emerges to the grim reality of [[Afral]]. As you all venture forth, the landscape becomes more manicured, the roads better maintained. You pass through prosperous villages, their fields tended by Large Rabbits and their skies dotted with well-fed Giant Owls carrying noble passengers.

As the city of [[Lyravelle]] comes into view, you find it indeed lives up to its name. It's a breathtaking metropolis of gleaming white stone, soaring spires, and intricate, almost mesmerizing architecture. Wide, clean boulevards are bustling with elegantly dressed citizens, merchants from distant lands, and the occasional pet cricket are being led through the streets, its chitin a pristine polished blue. The air is filled with the scent of exotic spices, fine perfumes, and the distant, melodic chime of bells. It feels like a beacon of civilization, prosperity, and peace.

Ask the players to share their thoughts on what they see and observe. What is it that each individual player is looking for?

Arriving around midday, the envoy turns to the three of you. “It seems we have made good time, her Ladyship does have a busy schedule so I ask that you arrive at the palace around supper time. She will be holding a feast in your honor and a room will be provided after the festivities have concluded.” With that, he turns and walks through the palace gate.

### A Feast fit for Heroes

Arriving back to the palace, you find hordes of people entering through the main gate, a large marble archway with runes carved into each voussoirs with a large Emerald gemstone set into the Keystone. A woman approaches you.

“Ah, the heroes from [[Afral]]! Welcome to [[Lyravelle]]. My name is Taniya Eldrida, you may also refer to me as the Chancellor’s Hand.”

“You must forgive the crude state of the outer cities; here, we prefer to handle threats with finesse, you see. Your accommodations are ready and a fresh set of clothes have been prepared for you. Although, I must insist you take a moment to rest—the Chancellor values a well-composed appearance at the evening's feast."

You are guided to a table at the head of the Grand Hall. Chancellor Valerius sits with Miss Eldrida to her right and a gruff stout Dwarf with scars covering his face and a Black gauntleted arm to her left. Continuing on sits a rather tall and poised dragonborn with scales of polished iridescent Sapphire. Past the Chancellor’s hand sit three empty seats. Lady Valerius gestures for you to sit.

The room falls silent as Taniya Eldrida rises from the table, offering a graceful, practiced smile to the hall. "Friends, esteemed colleagues, and honored guests. I ask for your attention. I have the distinct privilege of introducing the reason we are all here tonight, a woman whose wisdom is the rudder of our nation: the High Chancellor of the Republic of Aeridor."

"Tonight, we celebrate not a political victory, but the profound virtue of vigilance and civic courage."

A shadow cast not by foreign enemies, but by a handful of self-serving criminals who betrayed the trust placed in them. (She raises his voice slightly) "Through their bold actions, they located the source of the plague—a massive, magically induced pollution of [[Afral]]'s primary water source—and they cleansed it! They took decisive, necessary action to seal off the criminal network's hidden supply lines, severing the black market's roots. And, most crucially, they retrieved the evidence needed to ensure that justice, under the watchful eye of the Republic, could be served." (The Chancellor pauses, Her eyes meeting yours.) "They remind us that the strength of Aeridor rests not only on the spires of our capital, but on the integrity of our people. They took a dangerous, localized situation and, through sheer bravery and competence, delivered the evidence of guilt into the hands of the authorities. ### Recap

The last session marked the final, explosive end of your time in [[Afral]]. You've climbed from the filth of the sewers to the gilded halls of the capital. Your Wizard friend, [[Ignis Vayne|Master Ignis]], revealed the ledger you stole was a documented network of treason pointing to a more complex web of near-do-wells. It confirmed that the merchant [[Nadar]] was merely something called a "Weaver", likely someone low ranked in this mysterious cult, taking orders from a higher power known as 'The Spinner.' Before you could act on this, an envoy from [[Lyravelle]] arrived, bringing a summons from High Chancellor [[Lady Aurelia Valerius]]. You accepted the invitation, leaving the grim yet hopeful people of [[Afral]] behind for a journey through the prosperous, manicured heart of the Republic.

Arriving in [[Lyravelle]], you were thrust immediately into the center of attention. After a brief rest, you attended a grand feast held in your honor, where the Chancellor, Lady Valerius, delivered a public speech. She reframed your chaotic actions—the explosions, the sewage, the fights—as "vigilance and civic courage," solidifying your heroic status while carefully sanitizing the official record. You met the most powerful figures in the Republic, including the imposing Lord General [[Kaelen Volkov]] and [[Verso Calvain|Verso]]’s own estranged family, House Calvain. The evening ended with the Chancellor awarding you a sack of gold and the deed to a townhouse, a magnificent reward that serves as a beautiful, very public anchor in the very city you find yourselves in.

### Dreamscape of [[Noxervus|Liora]]

Making your way to the Sunstone Promenade, several trees begin to glow faintly. Rolling, silvery fog manifests around the plants. The air suddenly smells not of busy streets and greasy street food, but of warm summer breezes and honeyed wildflowers.

A tear in reality opens before you, blooming outward like a drop of ink in clear water. Stepping through, the bustle of [[Lyravelle]] vanishes, replaced instantly by the hushed, melodic thrum of nightly insects and the faint strumming of distant, unseen instruments.

You stand now in [[EverBloom]]. The air here is unnaturally warm and humid. Massive bioluminescent plants pulse with a soft, rhythmic light, casting long, dancing shadows. Above you hangs a moon that never sets—currently resting in a crescent phase, reflecting the pensive mood of the Archfey. Nearby, a river of pitch-black water cuts through the garden; yet, looking down, the aquatic creatures glowing beneath the surface shine so brightly that the ebony water seems perfectly clear.

To the periphery of your vision, the lush garden gives way to something older: dark shadows that stretch out into an expansive desert of silvery mist and dark sand. It is the domain of the Veil, a haze that threatens to dissolve the senses of anyone who wanders too far.

From the expanse of the silver desert, the mist begins to swirl, thickening into a pillar of soft, grey light. It doesn't simply part; it condenses, knitting together until a shape stands before you.

The Owlin Lord stands tall, his plumage a gradient of twilight blues and soft greys that seem to ripple like smoke. His large, amber eyes hold the reflection of the endless moon, but they are heavy with a quiet sadness.

He steps from the dark sands onto the lush grass of the garden, his foot falls making no sound. He ignores the others for a moment, his gaze fixed entirely on the trembling Harengon. He kneels, bringing himself to eye level with her, and extends a wing, the feathers brushing her cheek like a passing breeze.

"My little wanderer," he speaks, his voice low and resonant, like the hum of the distant insects. "You have carried such a heavy burden so far from home. But you can let it go now. The cold cannot find you here. We are together again, and the mist will hold you until you are whole."

The tender silence between the towering fey and the ranger is suddenly shattered by a loud, melodious splash and a burst of uncontainable giggling.

From the branches of a massive, glowing willow overlooking the black river, a figure drops down, landing with surprising grace in a pile of luminescent petals near the kneeling Prince.

A Satyr bounds up, shaking leaves from her spiraling horns and flicking water from her fur. She grins, her eyes bright with mischief, completely ignoring the somber gravity of the moment. She nudges the towering Owlin Lord with her hip.

"Oh, Papa, you're going to bore her to death before the curse even gets a chance!" she teases, her voice light and chiming. She turns to the trembling Harengon and throws her arms wide. "[[Noxervus|Liora]]! Look at you! All gloom and doom and 'woe is me.' We simply cannot have that. We have catching up to do, and I have found the most interesting shiny rock since you left!"

## An Innocent Man's Solace

With a heavy, grinding groan, the final stone door gives way. You step out of the damp, miserable sewer and into a surprisingly warm, hidden sanctuary. Your boots sink into a thick, plush rug. The room is modestly furnished, offering a simple cot, a comfortable couch, and a small wooden coffee table. A circular stone grate in the wall allows a gentle, fresh breeze to filter in, carrying the comforting, musky scent of warm, spiced Aurora-Pip mead.

Commander Dalram steps in behind you, and as the heavy stone door seals shut, you see decades of tension finally leave the dwarf's shoulders. He unclasps his heavy armor, letting it hit the floor with a dull thud, and sinks onto the edge of the cot, burying his scarred face in his hands. He is safe, but the reality of his situation is just setting in.

Resting neatly on the coffee table is a rolled parchment, tied delicately with a neat bow. Unfurling it, the script provides a brief, plain message:

_"Rest here. We will make contact soon. But the Spiders' web stretches far beyond this mountain. Decide your next move, and do not linger long."_

### DM Notes & Mechanics: The Tanyia Dilemma & Next Steps

This section is completely open for roleplay. The adrenaline of the escape is over, and the players finally have a safe space to speak their minds and talk with Dalram.
