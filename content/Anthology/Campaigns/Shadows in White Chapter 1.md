# Prologue

In the Nation of Aeriador, people live happy, thriving lives. The nation strives for excellence and promotes rich culture and trade. While each major city is distances apart, each fulfills a unique and vital role in making Aeriador a place of wonder, delight, and peace. From the soaring spires of [[Lyravelle]], where diplomacy and commerce flourish, to the rugged, mineral-rich mountains surrounding [[Ironpeak]], and the arcane academies nestled in [[Silverwood]]'s ancient forests, a vibrant tapestry of cultures thrives. Magnificent Giant Robins soar through the skies as mounts, while tiny, delicate elephants roam the undergrowth, embodying a world where the familiar is delightfully inverted.

Yet, beneath this gleaming facade, a subtle disquiet stirs. Whispers of unseen hands guiding the decisions of powerful figures echo through the grand capitals, and an undercurrent of corruption flows through the very institutions meant to uphold justice and order. While the Republic's leaders appear to govern with wisdom and strength, a discerning eye might catch glimpses of a deeper influence, a hidden web of power that seeks to control the very destiny of Aeriador from the shadows. The peace is maintained, but at what unseen cost.

In the Grand Republic of Aeriador, your journey will begin as local heroes, tackling the immediate crises that plague the everyday folk. Your courageous deeds, however, will draw the attention of powerful figures, pulling you into the gilded halls of the capital cities. There, you will discover that the Republic's celebrated peace and prosperity are built upon a fragile foundation, subtly undermined by a pervasive corruption. As you delve deeper, you will uncover the insidious truth: a secret cabal of puppet masters manipulates the very leaders of Aeriador's grand cities, pulling strings from the shadows to serve their own enigmatic agenda. The fate of this magnificent nation, and the lives of its people, will ultimately rest on your ability to expose and dismantle this hidden power.

## Metamorphosis of Afral

### Recap

Over the course of the week following the well cleansing and the tunnels being sealed, Afral undergoes a noticeable and profound transformation. The change isn't instant—there are still rubble and repair crews—but the spirit of the town is lifted.

You notice children running freely in the streets, their mothers no longer clutching them with fevered anxiety. The beer at the Red Tigress Inn tastes crisper, and the bread baked by the gnome, Kaelia, seems to have a new, robust quality. The ever-present, low-grade malaise that once seemed to hang over the common folk has lifted.

### Cyrille’s Cottage

She lived in a simple cottage sized home. Bookcases line the walls with a small bedroom/study tucked away in the far back right corner. A very basic kitchen in good order looks like it has barely been touched in months. Near the entryway is a white brick fireplace surrounded by large round chairs where a body sits poised.

You find the body kneeling, head bowed, hands open and empty, placed neatly in their lap. The body is pristine, with no signs of struggle, except for a lethal wound across her chest. They were an elderly human woman with long red hair and a towering muscled build. Her name was Cyrille Iasbex.

Despite the simple facade, the room feels unnaturally quiet, devoid of the normal household hum. The chairs are subtly scorched, hinting at a desperate, recent struggle near the fireplace. The bookcases are the most personal touch, filled not with fictional stories, but with dense, technical manuals on advanced navigation, wind patterns, and the maintenance of magical flight beacons. A few identical trophies sit collecting dust, one reads Champion of House Kestrel’s Annual Hide & Seek competition dated 11th of Crystalis 576.

On the floor near the fireplace, a few critical pieces of evidence lay scattered

- DC 17 Investigation/Perception Check: The Logbook: A small, leather-bound flight log rests open, containing Cyrille's meticulous, tiny script detailing the projected arrival and departure times for Giant Robin transport fleets over the next week. A character with a high Investigation check will note that two of the scheduled arrival times for high-priority shipments have been scratched out and replaced with new, earlier times.

- DC 12 Nature check: The kitchen, though simple, is stocked with enough non-perishable goods and preserved foods to last several months, suggesting Cyrille was preparing for a prolonged absence or expected some form of extended isolation—a peculiar habit for a working coordinator.

- DC 18 Perception check: The front door was forced open, evidenced by the hole where the door knob would be. You find a single, microscopic, obsidian-black shard embedded in the wooden doorframe, easily missed by the official search. This shard is utterly foreign to the materials found in the house.
