# Overview

Can often be found near his home of [[Veridian Hearth]] tending to his flock. If he isn't there, chances are he has traveled North to [[Port Azure]] to barter livestock. A few locals know him and often stop by when he is in town to chat.

## Appearance

A tall green elf, [[Elred]] can often be seen tending to his animals out in the fields. To keep the sun at bay, he wears a long green cloak that matches his Satchel bag. His slim build can deceive the average onlooker to reveal that while not physically strong, it can take a lot to bring this towering farmer down.

# History

Centuries ago, before the Grand Republic of [[Aeriador]] was truly formed, the land was wilder, its primordial magic raw and untamed. This nexus was not just a powerful ley line; it was a fundamental anchor point for the natural world, subtly influencing the unique, inverted ecological balance that would one day define Aeridor – the very source that ensures Giant Robins can soar, Pocket Elephants can roam, and Giant Spiders maintain their natural, if intimidating, place in the ecosystem. The strange visions that plague [[Elred]] are not just resurfacing memories of his youth; they are dire, fragmented warnings from the nexus, showing glimpses of ecological catastrophe: Giant Spiders growing monstrously out of control, rivers boiling, forests consumed by unnatural blight, or the very magic of the land turning sour and destructive.
