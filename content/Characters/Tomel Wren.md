# History

Relationships
Tomel Wren:

Summary: Isolated in the gilded prison of his family estate, Verso found rare comfort in the company of a tinkerer named Tomel Wren, a kind-hear ted artificer from the village below the hill. Tomel began building prosthetic arms for him—clumsy at first, but improving year after year. These were tools of self-worth, of rebellion, and of survival. When the Calvains found out, Tomel was banned from the estate, and Verso was beaten and locked away. Still, the two met in secret, the bond between them becoming more familial than any Verso had ever known.

Details: Verso and Tomel originally met during [[Verso Calvain|Verso]]’s first escapade away from the estate. A chance meeting caused the two boys, around the same age of 8 at the time, to find each other. Tomel instantly became interested in Verso due to his unique disability, one which complimented his own craft. Verso, apprehensive at first, quickly warmed up to Tomel’s kind personality and lighthearted humor, the first he had truly experienced.

Around the age of 10, Tomel created the first of many prosthetic for Verso. The fingers would lock up in the cold weather, the elbow squeaked with every movement, and the straps rubbed Verso’s shoulders raw, but it was the closest he had ever come to feeling normal. Though Tomel was disappointed in his creation, Verso was grateful.

For the next several years, Tomel iterated upon his creation, improving upon it each time. Around the age of 14, the arm had been perfected. It was strong, it worked, and it truly felt like an extension of his body. Verso, with a childish confidence, thought that a creation like this might impress his family and inspire them to be more accepting of them. Upon showing it to them, the disgust that showed in their expressions was stagnant in the air, a vision that has stuck with him since. The Arm was destroyed and hidden. Security grew tighter, and Verso’s secret visits to Tomel were halted.

Stuck within his confines even more than before, Verso began to search for the arm, eventually finding it lazily hidden, a mere afterthought to it’s opposition. After some time, Verso found himself able to escape once again; perhaps security loosened or perhaps he adapted to their watch, he was unsure. He began visiting Tomel in secret once again, though more infrequent than before. Tomel never asked him what had happened, he simply continued to show the same kindness that he always had. The arm was repaired, improved, and stored secretly just outside of the Calvain estate for use during only his secret excursions.

One fateful night during his sixteenth winter, Verso followed a whisper in the woods while on his way to visit Tomel. A voice, like wind and honey, called him toward a ruined grove forgotten by time. There, he met [[Thalirien]] of the Pale Thorns, an Archfey with antlers of birch and a crown of moths. The being offered him a chance to change his fate. But as all fey bargains go, the price was hidden in the poetry. He accepted without fully understanding—and in that moment, his left hand twisted with pain. His index and middle fingers vanished, plucked by unseen thorns. Yet in their place, his prosthetic arm shimmered with fey sigils, awakening with unnatural grace and strength. Groaning in pain, a small, singular antler emerged from his forehead, leaving a symbolic mark of Thalirien's influence.

When he finally arrived, once again Tomel did not ask what happened. Utilizing parts from a previously failed arm, a prosthetic was crafted for his now missing fingers. The arm, now entangled into his own flesh by arcane vines, was met with immediate looks of disgust upon his return to the estate, worsened when his hand was observed. Once again, Verso was locked away tighter.

For the next 9 years (25 yrs old), Verso was never again able to escape. In this time, he trained and grew stronger. He began to hone his skill with a sword, and found himself slowly able to wield simple magic. When the opening arose and his strength had grown enough he escaped once again.

Not knowing where to go, he wandered to Tomel once again. Again, Tomel did not ask where he had been or what had happened, he simply embraced him as he always had. Their reunion, though brief, was warm with a brotherly love. Verso left him with a promise, “I have to leave this place for a while, hide away until I am stronger; but I will be back to see you again.”

A little over a year has passed since their last meeting.
