## Appearance

[[Verso Calvain|Verso]] is a tall, young looking [[Human|human]] male with medium length dirty blonde/light brown hair that is often tied back in a tight bun, braid, or ponytail. His skin is pale, giving the impression he has spent little time in the elements. Dark circles envelop his eyes and hollow, sunken cheeks give off a gaunt, lifeless appearance masking his otherwise traditional noble features. [[Verso Calvain|Verso]] often wears a leather coat and gloves, covering much of his skin, but if removed, a metallic right arm inscribed with fey runes would be revealed. The runes and gaps in the metal of the arm glow a dull, arcane purple. his left hand, when revealed, consist of only 3 natural fingers, with a simple metal and leather prosthetic replacing the middle and index fingers.

# History

Running away from a questionable home situation, questioning powers he does not understand, Verso is simply following the path that life leads him on with little to no motivation, no goals, and no concern for what comes next.

Verso Calvain was born into the illustrious House Calvain, a respected noble family known for their deep roots in courtly politics and their unwavering dedication to tradition. From the moment of his birth, Verso was seen as an ill omen—he entered the world missing his right arm. In a house obsessed with legacy, image, and perfection, this was more than a misfortune; it was interpreted as divine punishment.
Rumors whispered through the halls that Verso had been cursed, perhaps for a slight committed by the Calvains generations ago. Though no such claim was ever proven, the idea stuck.
His mother, Lady Marcella Calvain, a sharp-tongued woman who held herself with iron pride, treated Verso like a stain on her family’s tapestry. His father, Lord Ellion Calvain, was rarely present, often away on political missions or “diplomatic retreats” that were more scandal than service. His older brother, Caedric, a golden child with a knight's bearing and a politician’s tongue, openly mocked Verso’s weakness. His older sister, Serelise, cold and cunning, ignored him entirely unless it served her ambitions.
Isolated in the gilded prison of his family estate, Verso found rare comfort in the company of a tinker’s apprentice named Tomel Wren, a kind-hearted artificer from the village below the hill. Tomel began building prosthetic arms for him—clumsy at first, but improving year after year. These were tools of self-worth, of rebellion, and of survival. When the Calvains found out, Tomel was banned from the estate, and Verso was beaten and locked away. Still, the two met in secret, the bond between them becoming more familial than any Verso had ever known.
One fateful night during his sixteenth winter, Verso followed a whisper in the woods. A voice, like wind and honey, called him toward a ruined grove forgotten by time. There, he met Thalirien of the Pale Thorns, an Archfey with antlers of birch and a crown of moths. The being offered him a chance to change his fate. But as all fey bargains go, the price was hidden in the poetry. He accepted without fully understanding—and in that moment, his left hand twisted with pain. His index and middle fingers vanished, plucked by unseen thorns. Yet in their place, his prosthetic arm shimmered with fey sigils, awakening with unnatural grace and strength. Groaning in pain, a small, singular antler emerged from his forehead, leaving a symbolic mark of Thalirien's influence.
Since then, Verso has trained in secret. He learned to fight to earn his own agency—not as a knight like Caedric, but with raw grit and cleverness. He uses both steel and subtle magic, weaving illusions and charms from his pact with Thalirien and bolstering his blades strength. His warlock powers are less about destruction, and more about movement, misdirection, and insight—gifts stolen from the ever-shifting Feywild.
