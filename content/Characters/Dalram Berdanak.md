# History

- Being blackmailed by the Crimson Spiders with the secret of his illegitimate child, they force him to keep ore prices artificially low for Lyravelle's benefit. [Ironpeak](Ironpeak.md)'s powerful military is subtly guided to "protect" specific Spider-controlled trade routes, or to engage in border skirmishes with a third city to divert attention from internal issues.
- Dalram later found the cold body of Reyhana Eldrida there and his heart broke. With nothing left to protect except his people, he became subservient to the Spider’s cause. Over 20 years later, the Crimson Spiders are using the daughter he thought murdered as another piece to lord over him. Little did they know that this was his tipping point. He began to rebel against them in little ways.
- Father to his illegitimate daughter Taniya Eldrida
