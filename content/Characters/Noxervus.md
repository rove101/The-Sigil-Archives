# History

## Preparing for the Mortal Plane

When [[Vespera]] saw a vision of terror and evil befalling the [[mortal plane]], [[Noxervus]] was trained to be a [[diplomat]] so she may return to the [[mortal plane]].
When undergoing this training, [[Noxervus]] was introduced to [[Aethelgard]], the [[Archfey]] of [[Everbloom]]. After meeting [[Aethelgard]], this is when [[Noxervus]] would cover her ear scar in her presence. Other [[fey]] creatures in [[Everbloom]] also underwent this training; some made fun of [[Noxervus]], but others were close with her, putting flowers and trinkets on her antlers as they grew. [[Umbrax]] continued [[Noxervus]]’ training in less frequent doses, sharing his plan to overtake [[Aethelgard]] as the new [[Archfey]] of [[Everbloom]]. When it was time for [[Noxervus]] to leave for the [[mortal plane]], her friends gave her some [[trinkets]] to remember [[Everbloom]], and [[Umbrax]] walked with her to the edge of the [[planes]] to remind her of her second mission to usher lost souls to him.
