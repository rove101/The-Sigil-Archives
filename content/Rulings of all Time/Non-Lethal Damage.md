Damage applied to a creature that results in it being reduced to zero hit points will be required to make [[Death Saving Throws]]. If the damage is that which is considered [[Non-Lethal Damage#Damage types|Non-Lethal]], a player may declare intent to perform "non-lethal" damage. The damage to a creature instead forces the creature to 1 [[HP]] and gives the [[Unconscious]] condition for a duration of at least 8 hours. The condition may be removed from the creature if another creature uses their [[action]] to shake or slap the creature awake or if the [[Unconscious]] creature takes damage.

# Damage types

- Cold
- Force
- Psychic
- Thunder
- Bludgeoning
