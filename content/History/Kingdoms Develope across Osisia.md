# Overview

The planet [[Oisia - Prime Singularity|Oisia]] is discovered to have sentient primitive life in the form of anthropomorphic humanoids. Further study has reveled that trees and similar naturally occurring items have suffused with magic. The once thought anomalous planet seems to have stabilized enough to develop life beyond Slime and other Monstrous creatures. Several empires have already begun to study utilizing autonomous constructs they are calling probes.
