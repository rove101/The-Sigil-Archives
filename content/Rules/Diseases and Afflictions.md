Diseases hold an odd position in fantasy worlds. They are meant to be as dangerous as real-world epidemics; however, magical cures and remedies are readily available to characters at lower levels, making them difficult to use as either an interesting game mechanic or a viable storytelling hook or plot device. In fact, 2024 5e rules barely mention diseases as a game element.

Grim Hollow, however, wants diseases to be a relevant— and even crucial—factor in adventures and campaigns set in Etharis. The Weeping Pox is an important piece of lore in Etharis's history, so to eliminate them completely flies in the face of everything Grim Hollow is about.

As a way of making diseases, contagions, and afflictions a viable tool for GMs to tell stories, use the following rules.

# Natural Diseases

Various naturally occurring viruses and bacteria regularly rampage through Etharis, causing both isolated cases of a disease as well as more dire epidemics. These diseases, however, can be combated with normal medicine, as well as eradicated by magic. Sometimes the symptoms of even the most contagious and debilitating disease can be managed with lower-level magic, or cured completely with spells such as Lesser Restoration.

## Rules for Natural Diseases

For a standard, nonmagical disease or affliction, an exposed creature can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. For harsher diseases, the DC could increase as necessary to enforce the deadly nature of the disease.

On a successful save, the character might show very mild symptoms but suffer no other game-mechanically relevant effects. On a failed save, the character gains 1 Exhaustion level.

After two days have passed, the character can repeat the saving throw. On a failed save, they gain another Exhaustion level. If they did not complete a Long Rest over that 2-day stretch, they have Disadvantage on the check. On a success, they have resisted the disease.

This process continues until the character has either resisted the disease by succeeding on a saving throw, been cured via medicine or magic, or die from gaining 6 Exhaustion levels.

A GM can determine the ways in which Medicine checks or other healing efforts might succeed. It's important to let characters with proficiency in skills or tools use those avenues for healing.

# Magical Diseases and Contagions

The Core Rules for 2024 fifth edition have rules and examples of magical contagions, but the rules here could supplement them to show the particularly deadly place these contagions hold in Etharis.

Magical diseases and contagions have a similar effect and method of resolution to natural diseases. However, there may be affects in addition to Exhaustion levels. The DC for resisting the magical diseases is 15 instead of 10, and typical magic or rest cannot remove Exhaustion levels.

The other rules for suffering from and resting the magical diseases and contagions should follow the pattern set in the previous section.

# Curse Diseases and Afflictions

Even more virulent, pervasive, and deadly are curse diseases and afflictions. These are not just magical in nature, they are harder to shake and have more potentially dire consequences if not healed in a reasonable time. The Weeping Pox is an example of a curse disease that is highly unlikely to be cured easily, even with magic.

Unlike the lesser varieties of diseases and magical contagions, curse diseases and afflictions cannot be cured by Lesser Restoration or other lower-level spells or powers. Magic on the level of Wish is needed to even have a chance of curing such afflictions, and even then it is not a sure thing.

> ## The Weeping Pox

An example of a curse disease in Etharis is the Weeping Pox. A person subjected to the disease must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw once each day they are exposed. If they succeed, they are safe for 24 hours. On a failed save, they have the affliction and begin to show symptoms within 24 hours.

> At first a sick person will be subject to fever causing sharpened senses, overexcitement, and a sense of wellbeing. For the first three days, creatures suffering from the Weeping Pox must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw each day. If any of those saving throws fail, the creature reaches critical stages at the end of those 72 hours.
>
> After 72 hours, these original symptoms transition to delirium, burning fever, insatiable thirst, and weltering lesions that have given the illness its name. Beyond this, nearly all who contract the disease die withing the next 72 hours. Most people succumb to the pox within the week, while the most resilient may hold on for almost two weeks.
