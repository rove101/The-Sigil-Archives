# Leveling

Leveling up in Dungeons and Dragons is how players measure their strength and determine what they can and can't do. This is done by gaining proficiency, abilities, health points and more spell slots or maneuver.

## Gaining a Hero Level

This is the traditional level that most people expect. When a player gains enough XP they get a [[Leveling#Gaining a Hero Level|Hero Level]]. Like a standard [[Leveling#Gaining a Class Level|Class Level]], this gives you bonus Health Points or HP but that is where similarities end. A [[Leveling#Gaining a Hero Level|Hero Level]] instead gives a player the option of a proficiency, a language, or a feat/boon.

## Gaining a Class Level

- 10 points to level a class.
- Morning and evening phase is one downtime point each per downtime phase.
- 1 business week to level with no modifiers.
- Subtract relevant ability mod from point requirement.
- teacher halves requirement rounded up
- X gp per point to level a class (100 gp?)
  - example:
    - monk level with +5 dex mod requires 5 downtime points.
    - With no teacher, player pays 500 gp and spends 2.5 days (3 morning and 2 evening downtime points). This leaves other half of week (downtime phase) for other events.
    - With teacher player, player pays 500 gp and spends 1.5 days (2 morning and 1 evening downtime points). This leaves 3/5ths of week (downtime phase) for other events.

## Multi-Classing

To gain a level in a different class requires a teacher with the relevant skills.
