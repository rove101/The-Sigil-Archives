The [[Kliean Technocracy]] maintains this taxonomy to ensure logistical precision across the [[Slip-Space]]. These classifications are defined by their displacement, tactical utility, and the complexity of their integrated Technika. The **[[Hyperion]] & [[Aegis]] Intergalactic Colony Ship** remains the most significant observed anomaly in the history of the Vale. Originally constructed as the "sum total of all knowledge," the vessel was designed to preserve the civilizations of the far reaches of the galaxy.

- **Technological Heredity:** Records indicate the **[[Hyperion]]** was an escape vessel fleeing an existential threat described in salvaged logs as a "corrupt ice Giants" or "Frost Giant" of immense power.
- **The [[Oisia - Prime Singularity|Oisia]] Incident:** During its intergalactic transit, the vessel sustained catastrophic internal hull compromise to sectors four through nine. Following the activation of "Protocol Zero," the ship performed a forced atmospheric entry and crash-landed on the planet [[Oisia - Prime Singularity|Oisia]]. This event catalyzed the birth of the [[Kliean Technocracy]] and the scavenged socio-technological culture known as the [[Kingdom of Scrap]].
- **The Cradle:** Located deep within the ship’s primary superstructure, **The Cradle** houses high-output genetic facilities. These laboratories were engineered to preserve species data and generate clones, ensuring biological survival despite the "Great Crash."
- **Exodus (Exo) Infrastructure:** The [[Exodus project]] represents the infrastructure required for space faring survival—specifically the launch platforms and stations. These are managed by the [[Moses]] faction, a leadership cadre dedicated to launching infected species into [[Slip-Space]] to avoid total extinction.

## Cruiser Class

Very common fleet ship. Mostly seen performing long range transportation missions. Standard ship in the [[Alliance]] fleet, capable of holding it's own unless faced with overwhelming odds.

- [Krait MkII](https://elite-dangerous.fandom.com/wiki/Krait_Mk_II)
- [Alliance Crusader](https://elite-dangerous.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_Crusader)

Battle cruisers represent the standard for tactical engagement within the Vale, reflecting a fundamental split in naval philosophy between durability and environmental adaptability.

### Two Types

There are currently two class of Cruiser Class in mass production. The Farragut manufactured by [[Composite Industries]] and the Majestic manufactured by [[Arkwrights United]]. There are, of course, many other Cruisers in production but they are produced by private corporations and as such can be less than reliable when it comes to mass production. The civil market does often purchase from them to great effect though.

**Farragut:** The Farragut is the pinnacle of heavy armor battle cruisers. Designed for front line attrition, it prioritizes maximum hull plating and massive firepower over kinetic agility. It is the preferred vessel for siege operations.

**Majestic** In contrast, the Majestic is a nimble "inside-space" cruiser. It is engineered for high maneuverability within complex astral environments such as debris fields or dense planetary rings where the Farragut’s bulk would be a liability.

# Specialized Classifications

## Warships & Interdictors

### Interdictor Class Vessels

The Interdictor Class fulfills a critical area-denial role within the Fleets. These vessels utilize specialized containment field mechanics to generate gravity wells or energetic dampening fields. These fields prevent hostile vessels from escaping into Slip-Space, effectively trapping them for neutralization or boarding, and are essential for enforcing naval blockades.

## Interdenominational & Astral Transit Craft

### Solar Sail Vessels

Drawing inspiration from ancient nautical aesthetics, these vessels harness "Ethereal winds" and magic particles to navigate the vacuum without conventional fuel. They are primarily utilized for exploration and civilian transit within the [[Ethereal Sea]] of [[0 Inner Planes index|The Vale]].

### FTL Slip-Space Vessels

These craft are designed to navigate without the use of [[spatial rings]] to enter [[Slip-Space]] for rapid inter-system transit. However, [[Wayfarers]] are warned that [[Slip-Space]] travel is highly dangerous and often restricted. It borders the [[Far Realm]], exposing the crew to forces of chaos and corruption that can unravel the sanity of biological entities and the integrity of [[Technika systems]].

# Propulsion Systems & Technika Specifications

The [[Sigil Order]] recognizes four primary propulsion methodologies, each with distinct mechanical profiles:

1. **Solar Sails:** Passive propulsion that catches [[Weave]]'s particles emitted by a system’s star. This is the most efficient travel method across the [[Ethereal Sea]].
2. **Ionized Pulse Engines:** The standard propulsion for fleet maneuvers, providing reliable sub-light thrust through the ionization of noble gases.
3. **Gravity Damper:** Whether it was the office party punch or the list of 'ideas that sound dumb but could work', Engineers at [[Planet Shifters Inc]] developed the theory and thruster assembly that moves the universe relative to the ship. One engineer said it was "like a spider suspended in the air pushing a paper across a table." A demonstration was attempted to be put together but they couldn't find the spider.
4. **Plasma Fusion:** The team behind the Plasma Fusion drive asked themselves "what happens when you make the heat that propels a [[Planar Vessel|Star Ship]] even hotter?" That led them to create a feed back system that injects super heated particles back into the jet cone of a regular thruster. The result, while inefficient at lower thrust, generates a Fractal scaling curve of propulsion. The technical upper limit on the thruster array is theorized at sub infinite but no one is willing to test the theory.

## Slip-Space Mechanics:

Entering [[Slip-Space]] requires specific harmonic frequencies to be calibrated every fourth rotation of a quantum fused particle. That is to say very complex and very fast. Thanks to the engineers at [[Meridian Transit Laboratory]], the [[spatial ring]] has been developed and deployed across the stars.

Navigation through [[Slip-Space]] involves severe time-dilation. Due to the warping of the dimensional fabric, hours of travel within the corridor may equate to years in real-space.

### Navigation Warning:

[[Waystone Keys]] deciphered by the [[Silverfronds Society]], [[Waystone Keys]] are volatile metaphysical anchors used for planar travel. They are extremely dangerous, often triggering a vortex that can tear the fabric of the [[Weave]]. Use of these keys near a [[Chronal-Vortex]] requires a power anchor to prevent the ship and crew from being lost to permanent planar drift.
