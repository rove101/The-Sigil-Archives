# Inner Planes index

> [!info]
> Each Planet has a core essence. A wisp of power siphoned from a different plane. These are the starting point for each planet and how it was formed by a god. But essence is only the beginning. Matter, physical stuff is needed to give shape and anchor that essence to [[0 Inner Planes index|The Cosmic Vale]]. The gods used Oisia as a source of this matter. None of them questioned why the planet seemed a near infinite source of stuff for them to create with. All that they knew was that over time as they took, it would grow back stronger.
